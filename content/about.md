learning engineer, on AI agents on Google Cloud. A lot of that work is checking whether an agent's
answers are right.

Outside work, I pick a real problem, build an agent for it, put it online and measure how well it
does.

How I work:

- Every project has tests that check the agent's answers and the steps it took to reach them.
- When I write, it's about what I measured and what I found.

Certifications: Google Cloud Professional Machine Learning Engineer and Professional Data
Engineer (2026).
